ACCESSIBILITY

SafeWave should work for more people.

Accessibility is part of the product quality bar, not an afterthought.

⌨

Keyboard support

SafeWave includes visible focus states, keyboard-accessible player controls, a skip-to-content link, and keyboard search/player shortcuts where supported.

◐

Motion and contrast

The interface respects reduced-motion preferences in key animated areas and uses high-contrast controls against the dark SafeWave interface.

↗

Continuous improvement

Accessibility is part of launch QA. Issues discovered on real devices and assistive technologies should be treated as release defects and corrected.
