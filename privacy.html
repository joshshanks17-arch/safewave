PRIVACY

Privacy at SafeWave.

A plain-language summary of how the current SafeWave launch-candidate site handles data.

01

What this build stores

SafeWave currently stores playback preferences, favorites, listening history, queues, and locally created playlists in your browser using local storage. That information stays on the device/browser unless a future account service is introduced.

02

Accounts and analytics

This launch-candidate build does not provide working user accounts and does not include third-party analytics or advertising trackers. If those features are added, this notice should be updated before they are enabled.

03

Audio requests

Playing music requires your browser to request the audio and site files from the hosting provider. The hosting provider may maintain its own technical logs under its own policies.

Launch note: This notice describes the current static SafeWave build. Review it whenever hosting, analytics, accounts, payments, forms, or other data-processing features change.
