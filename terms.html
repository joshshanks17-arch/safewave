SITE TERMS

Simple rules for using SafeWave.

These terms describe use of the SafeWave website; music licensing is handled separately.

01

Using the site

You may browse and stream SafeWave through the features made available on the site. Do not interfere with the service, attempt unauthorized access, or misuse site functionality.

02

Music rights are separate

Access to a track on the website does not by itself grant permission to publish, redistribute, resell, claim ownership of, or otherwise exploit that track. Any creator-use rights must come from the license terms applicable to that music.

03

Launch candidate

SafeWave is still being prepared for public release. Features may change, and unfinished account or commercial services should not be relied on until they are expressly launched.

Important: These site terms are a launch-readiness baseline, not a substitute for attorney-reviewed commercial music-license terms. Final creator licensing should be approved before SafeWave markets tracks for publication or commercial use.
